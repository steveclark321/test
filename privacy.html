Privacy Policy

How Trucking24Fit collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website or contact our team.

Effective date: November 28, 2026

Trucking24Fit (“Trucking24Fit,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the website at trucking24fit.org and related business communications from our location at 24820 NE 135th Lane, Fort McCoy, FL 32134.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name, company name, email address, and phone number
  • Shipment details such as pickup and delivery locations, freight type, equipment needs, weight, pallet count, and dates
  • Messages and documents you send through our contact or quote forms
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device type, and pages visited

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Respond to quote requests and general inquiries
  • Coordinate transportation and logistics communications
  • Improve website performance and customer service
  • Maintain business records and meet legal or operational requirements

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate our website, email delivery, or business systems, and when required by law, regulation, or valid legal process. Freight details may also be shared with operational partners as needed to evaluate or fulfill a transportation request.

4. Data Retention & Security

We retain business inquiry and shipment-related information for as long as reasonably needed for operations, customer follow-up, and recordkeeping. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information submitted through our website.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use essential cookies and standard hosting or analytics tools to understand site performance and improve user experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided to us, subject to legal and operational retention requirements. Contact us at contact@trucking24fit.org.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our website is intended for business users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above will be revised when changes are posted.
