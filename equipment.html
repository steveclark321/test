Equipment Built for Commercial Freight

Selecting the right equipment protects product integrity and improves loading efficiency. Share dimensions, weight, and handling needs when you request a quote.

Dry Van Trailers

Enclosed 53-foot dry vans for general freight, palletized goods, packaged products, and standard commercial shipments that need full weather protection from origin to destination.

  • Trailer length 53 ft
  • Interior width 102 in
  • Interior height 110 in
  • Door opening 110 in H × 99 in W
  • Cubic capacity Approx. 3,800–4,000 cu ft
  • Max payload Up to 45,000 lbs
  • Loading Rear dock / warehouse
  • Best for General & palletized freight

Flatbed Trailers

Open-deck flatbeds for freight that requires side, top, or crane loading—including construction materials, steel, machinery, crated equipment, and oversized pieces within legal limits.

  • Deck length 48 ft (53 ft available on request)
  • Deck width 8 ft 6 in
  • Deck height Approx. 60 in
  • Max payload Up to 48,000 lbs
  • Securement Straps, chains & tarps as required
  • Loading Side / top / crane
  • Best for Construction & industrial freight

Refrigerated Trailers

Temperature-controlled 53-foot reefers for freight that must stay within a defined climate range. Setpoints and unit readiness are confirmed before dispatch for every temperature-sensitive load.

  • Trailer length 53 ft
  • Interior width 98–100 in
  • Interior height 105–107 in
  • Temp range −20°F to 80°F
  • Cubic capacity Approx. 3,500–3,700 cu ft
  • Max payload Up to 43,000 lbs
  • Monitoring Reefer unit setpoint & run verification
  • Best for Temp-sensitive commercial freight

Box Trucks

Straight box trucks for local and regional commercial deliveries, smaller freight profiles, and facilities where a full tractor-trailer is not practical. Liftgate service available when requested.

  • Box length 26 ft
  • Box width 96–102 in
  • Box height 96–102 in
  • Cubic capacity Approx. 1,600–1,800 cu ft
  • Max payload Up to 10,000–12,000 lbs
  • GVWR class Up to 26,000 lbs
  • Access Urban & facility-friendly
  • Options Liftgate available on request

Day Cab Tractors

Day cab tractors for regional and short-haul operations where overnight sleeper accommodations are not required. Powered for standard fifth-wheel trailer combinations on Florida and Southeast lanes.

  • Configuration Day cab
  • Vehicle class Class 8
  • Coupling Standard fifth-wheel
  • Typical GCWR Up to 80,000 lbs
  • Engine class Heavy-duty diesel
  • Primary use Regional & local hauls
  • Trailer match Dry van, flatbed, reefer

Sleeper Cab Tractors

Sleeper cab tractors for long-haul lanes where driver rest accommodations support safe, compliant over-the-road operations across multi-state freight corridors.

  • Configuration Sleeper cab
  • Vehicle class Class 8
  • Coupling Standard fifth-wheel
  • Typical GCWR Up to 80,000 lbs
  • Engine class Heavy-duty diesel
  • Primary use Long-haul & OTR lanes
  • Trailer match Dry van, flatbed, reefer

Match Equipment to Freight Requirements

Not every shipment needs the same trailer or truck. Our team identifies practical equipment based on commodity type, dimensions, weight, loading method, and destination access.

  • Provide estimated weight and pallet count
  • Note temperature or tarping requirements
  • Share appointment or facility access limits
  • Flag oversized or high-value freight early
