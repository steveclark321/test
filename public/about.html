About Trucking24Fit

Built Around Reliable Freight Movement

Based in Fort McCoy, Florida, Trucking24Fit helps commercial businesses move freight with clarity, care, and operational discipline.

Who We Are

A Transportation Partner for Growing Businesses

Trucking24Fit provides dependable freight transportation and logistics support for shippers who need a professional partner—not just a one-time carrier.

From our operations base at 24820 NE 135th Lane in Fort McCoy, FL, we emphasize responsive communication, careful freight handling, and practical planning for Florida regional runs and longer multi-state lanes.

Our team works with commercial customers across construction, manufacturing, wholesale, retail distribution, building materials, and related industries that depend on predictable freight movement.

Mission

Our Mission

To provide professional trucking and freight transportation services that help businesses keep supply chains moving—through reliable execution, transparent communication, and a customer-first approach.

Vision

Our Vision

To be recognized as a trusted Florida-based transportation partner known for operational strength, safety-minded practices, and consistent service quality for commercial shippers.

Safety & Reliability

Commitment to Safe Transportation

Every freight move carries responsibility. Trucking24Fit prioritizes safe driving practices, equipment readiness, and careful freight securing as foundational parts of our service.

  • Pre-trip readiness and equipment awareness
  • Clear load details before dispatch
  • Respect for delivery windows and facility procedures
  • Prompt updates when conditions affect transit

Protection

Insurance Summary

Trucking24Fit maintains commercial transportation insurance appropriate for professional freight operations. Certificates of Insurance (COI) are available upon request for qualified business partners.

Commercial Auto Liability

Coverage for commercial vehicle operations supporting our trucking and freight transportation services.

Motor Truck Cargo

Cargo insurance to help protect eligible freight while in transit under Trucking24Fit transportation arrangements.

General Liability

Commercial general liability coverage supporting business operations and customer facility interactions.

To request a Certificate of Insurance, email contact@trucking24fit.org with your company name and certificate holder details.
